# Pith benchmark results

Suite A, handoff accuracy: a sender agent writes one message in the format under test; a fresh reader agent answers 11 multiple-choice questions about it, graded against the sender's notes by code. Sender and reader are the same model, with greedy decoding and thinking off. Local models ran in LM Studio (Q4 GGUF) on an RTX 3070 8 GB; hosted models ran through their provider's API. Method and how to reproduce: [HOW_TO_TEST.md](HOW_TO_TEST.md). Generated by `python bench/harness/leaderboard.py`.

Two case sets: the **original** cases (seed 2026), on which Pith 1.0 was tested and its failures studied, and **held-out** cases (other seeds), generated after Pith 1.1 was written, so 1.1 was not tuned on them.

## Reading these numbers

- **Message tokens** count only what the sender wrote. The Pith-with-skill arm also carries the skill text (about 2,500 tokens) in each agent's system prompt; the per-model `summary_h1.md` files report total tokens including it. With prompt caching that cost is paid once per conversation, not per message.
- **Evidence-status diff** is the change in how often the reader correctly knew whether a claim was verified or only believed. This is the accuracy gain Pith is designed for.
- **Intervals** are bootstrap over cases. With 60 cases, differences under about 10 points are within noise.
- Results are for 4-bit quantized local models with greedy decoding. Larger or hosted models may behave differently.
- Per-model details, with every transcript: `bench/results/handoff/<model>/`.
